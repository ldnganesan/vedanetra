Teaching note

Chapter 5: the Simmam (Leo) Ascendant — the Sun's only sign, a fixed royal fire sign of the lion. Frame it: self-reliant, commanding, proud; 'the lion hunts its own food and never eats a fallen creature.' No planet is exalted or debilitated here, so the sign 'gives one consistent result.' Same study method; confirm against the whole chart.

FOUNDATIONS

Simmam at a glance

Symbol

சிங்கம் — the Lion (four-legged). Hence 'Leo'.

Element / class

Fire-natured · cruel (krūra) / malefic sign · barren, manly, waterless

Modality

Sthira (ஸ்திர · fixed) · Oja (odd) · male sign

Order & body

5th sign of the zodiac · signifies the flank / upper belly (விலா)

Ruler / dignities

Sun (சூரியன்) — its only own sign, with mūlatrikoṇa here · none exalted/debilitated

Direction / colour

East (கிழக்கு) · red/rose · strong by day · śīrṣodaya (head-rising)

The Sun's only home — a fixed, royal fire sign: proud, self-reliant, commanding, hunting its own food.
Teaching note

Anchor the image: the lion, fire, the Sun, kingship, the upper belly. Fixed + fire + Sun predicts a proud, self-respecting, self-reliant, commanding nature that will not stoop or be servile. Note it's a day-strong, head-rising (śīrṣodaya) sign — stronger by day, like Gemini, unlike the earlier movable signs.

Full yogakāraka

Mars (4th kendra + 9th trikoṇa) is the great yogakāraka. Jupiter (5th + 8th) is a 'half' yoga-giver.

Bādhaka — 9th

Fixed signs take the 9th as bādhaka; Mars is bādhakādhipati, but its yogakāraka role largely does good.

Māraka — author's note

The author names the 3rd & 8th (Venus, Jupiter). Standard texts use the 2nd & 7th (Mercury, Saturn).

The marriage note

The 7th is Saturn's house, the Sun's 'enemy son' — so the wife often doesn't turn out satisfying.

Teaching note

Internalise the lordships. Mars owning 4+9 makes it the yogakāraka (mitigating its 9th-house bādhaka role too). As in Rishabam/Mithunam, flag the author's non-standard māraka (3 & 8) vs the textbook 2 & 7. And the structural marriage-difficulty note — 7th = Saturn, the Sun's enemy — recurs through the chapter.

What Simmam governs (kārakatva)

Kingship & valour

The Kshatriya; ruby & gems; hunting; victory in war; leadership & administration; briskness and swift action

Fire & mountain

Firewood, dried trees, electricity, wool; forest herbs; goods grown on mountains; self-grown groves & forest creatures

Teaching note

The through-line is the Sun and the lion: kingship, valour, hunting, leadership, fire, gems, and medicine. Very different from the Moon's watery Cancer. The health cluster is pitta/heat and the upper belly — recurring in every planet section as the 'hot constitution'.

THE NATIVE'S PORTRAIT

Classical view · two authorities

Phaladīpikā

Reddish eyes; a broad face & wide mouth; proud, with an "I am someone" feeling; valorous; angry without cause; fond of forests & mountains; separated from the mother; of firm intellect.

Satyāchārya

Of hard intellect; fond of meat; excess pitta; suffers losses yet has many undertakings; miserly but good-natured; fame-gaining; grieved over siblings; valorous; many wives; ailments of knees & teeth.

Teaching note

Read as period portraits. Both catch the proud, valorous, self-important, quick-tempered royal nature, and the mother-separation and sibling-grief motifs. The 'many undertakings but losses' note fits the ambitious, sometimes over-reaching lion.

THE NATIVE'S PORTRAIT

Classical view · Jātaka Alaṅkāram

Eats much; fond of temple abhiṣeka; of a benevolent heart; great hunger; courageous; of undiminished wisdom; bent on curing any disease at once and doing any task at once.

THE NATIVE'S PORTRAIT

From experience · the lion

Acts by its own counsel

Decides and acts on its own judgment; helps only the worthy; thoroughly dislikes sluggards; honest and word-keeping.

The lion hunts its own food

Holds labour as chief; dislikes eating by another's toil; stands on its own feet and won't seek borrowed support.

Bold, but not the aggressor

Faces anything boldly, fears nothing; won't start a fight but won't drop one that comes; tests before trusting.

Puts itself first

Esteems the father; shy in approaching women, connecting only when a woman holds on — and withdrawing once the purpose is served.

Teaching note

The author's 'lion' portrait — proud, self-reliant, self-respecting, bold, and refusing servility. The 'hunts its own food, never eats a fallen creature' image is the heart of it. Present the reserve-then-withdraw pattern with women descriptively.

THE NATIVE'S PORTRAIT

From experience · honour, temper, appetite

Settle it now

Consistent, pure-hearted, and openly expressive; anger flares and fades; no hidden grudge — everything settled then and there ('cash on hand').

Do not provoke

Not easily emotional — but provoked, they raise an uproar. Very reluctant to borrow, for fear a creditor might demand payment before others.

Honour above all

Cannot bear a word that touches their honour; act cautiously in public; won't easily be caught in trouble.

Appetite & health

Fond of six-taste, spicy, non-veg food, eaten without shyness; so indigestion & pitta troubles — jaundice, anaemia, migraine, piles.

Teaching note

The other half: the settle-it-now directness, the horror of a public debt, the sensitivity to honour, and the hearty appetite that brings pitta trouble. 'Do not provoke the lion' is the memorable teaching line. The marriage-difficulty (7th = Saturn) is channelled into earning instead.

Sun (Sūrya)

சூரியன்

Lord · own sign + mūlatrikoṇa

  • Hot-tempered, dismissive of others, self-important — 'nothing can be done without me' — with excessive self-confidence.
  • But if they fail, they don't tire — they try again and again, determined to win; of good earning ability; brisk and precise.
  • Non-servile: they finish tasks but won't be subservient; hair greys or balds early; heat-troubles (brain, piles, liver, pitta).
  • Strive tirelessly for a high state; of much desire but won't wander for it; hot constitution → incomplete satisfaction, unfretted.
Teaching note

The Sun in its own sign — the pure royal ego: proud, self-reliant, tireless, non-servile. Emphasise the 'try again until I win' persistence and the refusal to serve. The hot-constitution desire note recurs across the fire-sign planets.

Moon (Candra)

சந்திரன்

Friend · 12th lord

  • Waxing Moon: good mental courage and firm action; very tactful; successful, unafraid of expense; live for the present.
  • Fickle over women and free-spending for pleasure — but of good earning strength; much affection for the household's women; precise.
  • Somewhat hot-tempered; don't forget those who harmed them (though they don't take revenge).
  • Waning Moon: easily emotional, quick to anger, heedless speech, spendthrift; fond of fish & fowl; borrow even to win women's esteem; initial vigour declines.
Teaching note

The waxing/waning split again — waxing = courageous and tactful; waning = heedless and spendthrift. Always check the Moon's phase. As 12th lord it also colours the pleasure-and-expense theme.

Mars (Kuja)

செவ்வாய்

Friend · full yogakāraka · 4th & 9th

  • The full yogakāraka (kendra 4 + trikoṇa 9): of good professional strength and earning; prefers deeds that win fame.
  • But very intense anger — provoked, they lose all sense and may even strike; yet forget it at once and return to normal.
  • Fearless; act with a distinctive individuality; position, responsibility and public service seek them, discharged skilfully, without profit-seeking.
  • Enthusiastic in heroic deeds and sport; of much desire but incomplete satisfaction (hot constitution) → seek others, contract diseases, conceal it.
Teaching note

Mars is the FULL yogakāraka for Leo (owns 4+9) — the royal-warrior gift, same as in Cancer but here undebilitated. The reading is fame, command, courage — with the caution of fierce, blinding anger that passes as fast as it comes.

Mercury (Budha)

புதன்

Friend · 2nd & 11th

  • The 2nd-lord (dhana) in the Lagna gives excellent skill, intelligence and good anticipatory power; eager to know everything.
  • Details at the fingertips; quick understanding and swift, seldom-wrong decisions; great cautious conduct.
  • Masters of speech — hard to trap, quick to slip free; good earning ability, money always circulating around them.
  • Good artistic knowledge; of much desire (relishing & spending on women); attractive by dress & speech; arrange a good life by any means.
Teaching note

Mercury is a friend/benefic here (2nd lord of wealth in the Lagna) — sharp intellect, eloquence, and easy money. A genuinely strong, resourceful placement. This sets up the Budha-Āditya yoga (Sun+Mercury) in the conjunctions.

Jupiter (Guru)

குரு

Friend · half-yogakāraka · 5th & 8th

  • Cannot be underestimated: very magnanimous, detail-knowing, enthusiastic even in small matters, free of laziness.
  • Their 'big-man' bearing serves them well — a fear-mixed respect arises at the sight of them, and their authoritative speech is obeyed.
  • Inclined to public service & divine works; position and fame seek them, used well and without profit-seeking; selfless, charitable.
  • Honour jñānis, scholars and great men; though fickle over women, keep it very discreetly hidden and never compromise their honour.
Teaching note

Jupiter is a half-yogakāraka (5th trikoṇa + 8th) — a commanding, dignified, magnanimous presence whose very bearing wins deference. The 'fear-mixed respect' and 'authoritative speech' notes are pure royal Leo. Sets up the excellent Sun+Jupiter conjunction.

Venus (Śukra)

சுக்கிரன்

Enemy · 3rd & 10th

  • Of excellent professional strength and the power to finish tasks; always spotless in neat clothes, ornaments, perfumes.
  • Especially try to attract women; love the wife yet dominate her; while controlled by her, form ties with other women unknown to her.
  • Every night the wife must be beside them; they use both wife and others, spending on them without hesitation.
  • Must live comfortably and get every pleasure; hardship sours their face (even walking is a strain — must go by vehicle); not miserly.
Teaching note

Venus is an enemy (3rd & 10th) but still gives professional strength and a strong comfort-and-pleasure drive. The reading is luxury, aesthetics, and the marriage-plus-others theme. Present the desire material as the text's framing.

Saturn (Śani)

சனி

Enemy · 6th & 7th

  • Not special — Saturn reduces all of Simmam's distinctions; the naturally brisk native becomes a sluggard.
  • Careless, doing nothing responsibly; irritable over trifles; impatient; idling without direction and poking into needless trouble.
  • No zeal to earn; some live off the wife's earnings and are demeaned, losing Simmam's greatness.
  • Bad company breeds bad habits; desire low-class women's ties; suffer women-related diseases through excess — no good state to expect.
Teaching note

Saturn is an enemy (6th & 7th) and works directly against the sign's brisk, proud nature — turning the lion into a lethargic, discontented figure. This is the sharpest 'planet opposite to the sign's nature' case in the chapter.

Rahu (Rāhu)

ராகு
Enemy

Surface-skimming, money-driven.

  • Mostly surface-skimmers who act as if they know everything, without good thought or action.
  • To earn money they take any path — mostly crooked ones, fights, cheating-work; no clean speech or integrity; low-class ties.
  • A great desire to get rich fast through gambling, speculation, horse-racing, lottery and treasure-hunting.
  • For some, ties with widows and high-living women; their desire is excessive.
Teaching note

Rahu is an enemy here — the reading is a thick-skinned, money-at-any-cost, gambling-and-crooked-ways type. The cautionary node for Leo; present descriptively.

Ketu (Ketu)

கேது
Enemy

Cautious, deceiving, occult-edged.

  • Good subject-knowledge; very cautious and alert even in small matters; hard to deceive — but deceivers of others.
  • Every crooked path at their fingertips, used only when needed; 'nothing can be extracted from them'.
  • If trusted they do good; those who distrust them they entangle and watch sink; of god-devotion, yet fierce-deity or spirit contact.
  • Won't seek women but make women seek them; generally their functioning is unsatisfying and topsy-turvy.
Teaching note

Ketu is an enemy here — the sharp, secretive, hard-to-read, occult-tinged type, loyal to the trusting and dangerous to the distrusting. The mantra/spirit motif recurs across the nodes. Present descriptively.

PART 5

Conjunctions
in Simmam

The Sun joined with each planet — including the wise-and-eloquent Budha-Āditya

Sun+Mars · Sun+Mercury (Budha-Āditya) · Sun+Jupiter

Teaching note

The Sun is the lord, so the author frames combinations as 'Sun with X'. Preview: Sun+Mars, Sun+Mercury (Budha-Āditya) and Sun+Jupiter are the bright ones; Sun+Saturn and the nodes are the difficult ones.

CONJUNCTIONS

Sun + Moon · Sun + Mars

Sun + Moon

Astonishing skill; everything done with forethought; if they fail, they grieve but try again and win. Foodies (fond of fish); very quick to anger but forgetting at once; of much desire. As this falls near the new moon, the body runs hot 15 days, cool 15 — so allergy troubles; keen to advance, somewhat free-spending.

Sun + Mars

A special state: always brisk, of good administrative ability — an authority in their speech that makes subordinates fear them — with high earning. Attached to mother, siblings, father, but give the wife little importance; two fiery planets → hot constitution, so they compensate through earning. Foremost in public service, politics, divine works; fame-loving, keen on sport.

Teaching note

Sun+Mars (both fiery, Mars the yogakāraka) gives commanding administrative power and high earning, at the cost of warmth toward the wife. Sun+Moon gives skill and drive but an uneven hot/cool constitution. Both are strong, active configurations for the royal sign.

CONJUNCTIONS

Sun + Mercury · Sun + Jupiter

Sun + Mercury (Budha-Āditya)

A very lofty configuration: the 2nd-lord Mercury with the Sun gives immense skill, good professional profit and easy money-circulation; much subject-knowledge; masters of speech who win by talk yet keep their word; keen over (only quality) women; artistic, sociable, not miserly, clean in their ways.

Sun + Jupiter

Truly a lofty configuration: big, honourable men who won't do petty deeds or keep low company, achieving from where they sit; of administrative ability; selfless and public-minded; devout, doing divine works in the forefront; compassionate; position and fame seek them without their wandering; honour kept, desire discreet.

Teaching note

The two brightest Sun conjunctions. Budha-Āditya (Sun+Mercury) = the classic wise-and-eloquent combination, here with the wealth-lord. Sun+Jupiter = the noble, dignified, dharmic leader. Both keep honour and discretion — very royal Leo.

CONJUNCTIONS

Sun + Venus · Sun + Saturn

Sun + Venus

Proud, pompous, spending to be praised; of good professional strength (earning won't lack); free-spending, especially on women; high desire. With the pleasure-lord in the Lagna, 'enjoy while you can, O king' — but mostly out of tune with the wife, so they seek many women. Fire (Sun) meets water (Venus) → problems in sexual matters and incomplete satisfaction; yet a comfortable life.

Sun + Saturn

Cannot be called satisfying: contradictions even in speech; no self-confidence; concern and enthusiasm fade — careless, irritable, jumping up over trifles; bad habits and company arising of themselves. Through their low mentality even a beautiful wife seems ugly; mostly this affects their mental state, making them sluggards, all hesitation and timidity, life a burden.

Teaching note

Sun+Venus is comfort-and-pleasure driven with a fire-water clash in sexual matters; Sun+Saturn is the dispiriting combination — father and son, enemies — that undermines the sign's confidence and turns the lion timid. Handle the desire material as the text's framing.

CONJUNCTIONS

Sun + Rahu · Sun + Ketu

Sun + Rahu

Not a good state — reduces Simmam's greatness; crooked paths and crooked intellect; a craze to earn by any means, careless how; cheating-work and base gambling-ways; fear and submit to no one; no clean speech, integrity or trust; empty promises; obstinate, back-to-front action; very bad in women-matters; bad company arising of itself.

Sun + Ketu

Also not satisfying: cunning intellect and sharp knowledge turned to every crooked, well-planned, uncaught way; skilled at learning others' weaknesses and subduing them; make others believe they have great skills and catch them in their net; very stingy, getting all done at others' expense; use low women for their own advancement.

Teaching note

The nodal conjunctions with the lord Sun corrupt the royal nature — Rahu = money-crazed and crooked, Ketu = cunning and manipulative. The cautionary counterpart to the Mars/Mercury/Jupiter brilliance. Present descriptively.

CONJUNCTIONS

The multi-planet combines

With the Sun + Moon

+Mars: professional distinction, command, sport, family — honour never risked. +Mercury: immense knowledge, big profit from small effort, easy wins. +Jupiter: very special — noble, devout, just, honoured. +Venus: flawless — influence, comfort, pleasure.

Weaker Sun+Moon mixes

+Saturn: spoils the elevations; no satisfying wife; irresponsible; low-class ties. +Rahu: contradictory, bad/crooked ways, money the only aim. +Ketu: hard-pressing, unfathomable, 'one thing in, another out'.

The Mars/Jupiter combines

Moon+Mars+Jupiter special; Moon+Mars+Mercury flawless; Moon+Mars+Venus keeps comfort but heavy spending; Moon+Mars+Saturn breeds quarrels. Mars+Jupiter+Mercury/Venus flawless (high status, hidden private trouble).

The middling & weak

Mars+Jupiter+Saturn/nodes middling; Jupiter+Mercury+Venus good; Jupiter+Mercury+Saturn & Jupiter+nodes not special; Jupiter+Venus+Saturn & the Saturn-node mixes unsatisfying.

Teaching note

Summarise the author's combine guidance in four buckets. Headline: the Sun with the benefics (Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Moon, Venus) lifts to command and honour; Saturn and the nodes drag toward lethargy, crookedness and instability. The Mars-and-Jupiter combines are consistently strong.

SYNTHESIS

Putting it together

"The lion hunts its own food and never eats a fallen creature — so the Leo native stands on its own feet, and will not be servile."

Leo in one line: the Sun's proud, self-reliant, commanding fire — its warrior-strength carried by Mars, the full yogakāraka.

No planet is exalted or debilitated here — the sign gives one steady result; Saturn is its clearest undoing. Confirm with the whole chart.

Teaching note

Close the analytic arc. The Leo lesson: a proud, self-reliant, commanding fire sign whose strength is carried by Mars (the full yogakāraka) and whose clearest undoing is Saturn. Reiterate: single-factor readings are indicative; confirm with strength, aspects, vargas, daśā.

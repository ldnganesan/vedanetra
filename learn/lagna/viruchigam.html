Teaching note

Chapter 8: the Viruccigam (Scorpio) Ascendant — Mars's fixed water sign, "the scorpion." A lagna beset by functional enemies — the Moon debilitated, and even the lagna-lord Mars a functional enemy — but rescued by TWO saviours: the Sun (10th) and Jupiter (2nd & 5th, a dhana-yoga giver). Rāhu & Ketu are (disputedly) exalted here. Same study method; confirm against the whole chart.

FOUNDATIONS

Viruccigam at a glance

Symbol

தேள் — the Scorpion. It stings, and strikes from hiding.

Element / class

Water-natured (jala) · a fierce, malefic (pāpa/krūra) sign · ātma-jñāna · prolific

Modality

Sthira (ஸ்திர · fixed) · female sign

Order & body

8th sign of the zodiac · signifies the reproductive/generative organs

Ruler / dignities

Mars (செவ்வாய்) rules; Rāhu & Ketu (disputedly) exalted; the Moon debilitated

Direction / colour

North (வடக்கு) · ash / pale-golden · marks the twilight hour · śiras-udaya

Mars's fixed water sign of the scorpion — intense, secretive, skilled, and quick to sting, with the Moon fallen at its heart.
Teaching note

Anchor the image: the scorpion, water, Mars, the generative organs. Fixed + water + Mars predicts an intense, secretive, penetrating, skilled and self-guarded nature. The headline dignity fact — the Moon (the mind) DEBILITATED here — sets up the sign's inner tension.

The two saviours

Sun (10th, karma) & Jupiter (2nd+5th, dhana + trikoṇa — a dhana-yoga giver) are the yogakārakas that carry the sign.

Why the trials come

Mars owns the 6th (disease/enemies); the Moon (9th) is debilitated & bādhaka; Saturn (3&4) is an enemy; Venus (7&12) unsettles the marriage.

Bādhaka — 9th

Fixed signs take the 9th as bādhaka; the Moon is bādhakādhipati — harmful when waning or in 3/6/8/12.

Māraka — 3rd & 8th

The author names Saturn & Mercury (3 & 8). Standard texts use the 2nd & 7th (Jupiter & Venus).

Teaching note

Internalise the lordships and the sign's built-in trials: Mars owns the 6th, the Moon (9th) is fallen and the bādhaka, Saturn (3&4) is an enemy, Venus (7&12) unsettles marriage — all offset by the Sun (10th) and Jupiter (2&5). Flag the author's non-standard māraka (3 & 8) vs the textbook 2 & 7.

Teaching note

The through-line is Mars in the fixed water 8th sign: the deep, the hidden, the sharp and the transformative — mining, the occult, surgery, weapons, heavy machinery. The health cluster is the reproductive system. All recur in the planet sections.

THE NATIVE'S PORTRAIT

Classical view · two authorities

Phaladīpikā

Rounded calves & a broad chest; diseased; loses his father early; does cruel deeds; has the favour of the royal line; and has hands & feet marked by the lotus-line (padma-rēkhā).

Satyāchārya

Broad-built; orderly face & belly; cruel conduct; a pitta-heavy, yellowish body & eyes; slow to act yet walks fast; heads his town; a big family; a tormentor & big spender; not dear to the wife; enemies even in his own clan.

Teaching note

Read as period portraits. Both catch the intense, cruel-edged, disease-prone, town-leading, big-family, big-spending nature — and the strain in the marriage ('not dear to the wife') and with kin ('enemies in his own clan'). The lotus-line and royal favour mark distinction amid difficulty.

THE NATIVE'S PORTRAIT

Classical view · Jātaka Alaṅkāram

The harsh-natured scorpion. Born in Scorpio — very fond of mother & father; ever-loving to the wife; speaks happy words; completes without fail whatever he begins.

THE NATIVE'S PORTRAIT

From experience · the scorpion

The scorpion's sting

He speaks cuttingly of anyone; self-important; when angry, leaps without looking and pours out reckless, unbearable words — a master of sarcastic, hurtful speech.

Strikes from hiding

He cannot face an enemy directly; he retreats and takes revenge later — like the scorpion that hides at the lit lamp and stings when it is put out.

Worth three at a task

Courage a little short, yet he talks as if he'll do all himself — until a problem comes face to face, when he draws back. Pokes into needless matters.

Skilled, but self-undone

Many are gifted experts — mechanics who cure faults, inventors of new things — but haste, carelessness, quick anger and fierceness undo their chances.

Teaching note

The author's central image: the scorpion's sting. Cutting, self-important speech; revenge from hiding rather than open confrontation; genuine expert skill undercut by temper and carelessness. The 'strikes from hiding' image is the heart of it.

THE NATIVE'S PORTRAIT

From experience · desire, skill, creed

Desire runs high

Being the generative sign, the thought of desire is ever present; fond of coarse words; it is 'doubtful whether he is a faithful husband to even ten in a hundred.'

Both-bad, yet trusted

Cannot be crossed 'like a snake' nor trodden 'as rotten'; stays in a both-bad state — not to be trusted, yet arranging that one must trust him.

The expert's hands

Highly skilled workers of fine administrative sense — they do the intricate work others cannot, and cure faults others cannot find.

Live, don't suffer

Earners who spend lavishly and change their minds constantly; their creed: 'life is to be lived well, not suffered — enjoy it as far as it goes.'

Teaching note

The other half: the high desire, the 'both-bad but trusted' paradox, the genuine expert skill, and the 'live, don't suffer' creed. Handle the desire material maturely as the text's blunt period framing. Being a female sign, the author includes strong descriptions throughout.

NINE PLANETS · IN THE LAGNA
♂

Mars (Kuja)

செவ்வாய்

Lord — but a functional enemy

  • The lagna lord, but owning the 6th too, so a functional enemy: impatient, hasty, stubborn, quick-angered, speaking with a cunning mask.
  • They poke into needless matters and stir up confusion, earning a bad name — 'without aim, not to any purpose'; they cannot sit idle.
  • Fond of heroic deeds, sport, fights and disputes, unafraid, saying whatever is in mind — making many enemies; won't change their stand for anyone.
  • As a fiery planet in a watery, generative sign, desire runs high in both sexes; satisfaction is hard, so they seek many partners — inviting disease — and, spending beyond means, fall into debt.
Teaching note

Mars is both the lagna lord AND the 6th lord, so even the ruler is a functional enemy — the harsh, restless, quarrel-prone core of the sign. Note the fiery-planet-in-water-sign desire reading. Not a special single placement.

Sun (Sūrya)

சூரியன்

Friend · 10th lord · yogakāraka

  • The 10th (karma) lord in the lagna is a boon: brisk, quick, task-completing skilled experts, scholars and shrewd businessmen, good administrators.
  • Some win government posts, even high ones; they wish to know everything, details at their fingertips.
  • Earners who spend lavishly and enjoy life, yet care about saving and acquiring property; high desire, but they do not wander for women.
  • They form government ties, dislike crooked routes to fame, are mostly fair-minded, respect others' views but decide for themselves, and always want to be at the forefront.
Teaching note

One of the two saviours: the Sun owns the 10th, so Sun-in-lagna is a strong career signature — the fiery, skilful, administrative engine of the sign. A genuinely good single placement.

Moon (Candra)

சந்திரன்

Debilitated · 9th lord · bādhaka

  • The bhāgya-lord Moon, also the bādhaka, is debilitated with no strength in Scorpio — 'not to be called special.'
  • As the mind-significator it makes them mind-unsteady, agitated, unable to make quick firm decisions — so time passes and chances are missed.
  • Attachment to mother and father is reduced, but attraction to women heightened; as a watery planet in a water sign, the body runs cold and kapha-heavy.
  • High potency and skill in the art of love, ever needing a woman's nearness; from the unsteady mind come careless earning, constant worry, and a refusal of advice.
Teaching note

The chapter's key weakness: the Moon (the manas-kāraka) is debilitated AND the bādhaka. Its only redemption is a waxing Moon with neechabhaṅga. This fallen Moon is why 'mental agitation runs high' for the sign.

Mercury (Budha)

புதன்

Enemy · 8th & 11th lord · māraka

  • As the 8th & 11th lord — an unsteady planet, often retrograde or combust near the Sun — Mercury is not good in Scorpio.
  • In the lagna with dik-bala it fully overpowers them: no firm state, speech and thought changing back and forth; not to be trusted, and those who trust are dropped.
  • They talk tall ('bend the sky like a bow'); fraud and cheating come naturally; money is prime, earned any way.
  • Very bad in women-matters; skilled in the art of love, expert at charming and profiting from women; their creed: 'life is to be lived well, not suffered.'
Teaching note

Mercury is a functional malefic here (8th & 11th, and a māraka), unsteady and often combust near the Sun — the fraud-prone, untrustworthy, tall-talking placement. Present the descriptive material as the text's framing.

Jupiter (Guru)

குரு

Friend · 2nd & 5th lord · yogakāraka

  • Owning the 2nd (dhana) & 5th (trikoṇa), a dhana-yoga giver: they look big, and to protect honour will spend even by borrowing.
  • Jupiter keeps them always in a big-man's station ('must be on top'), and aspecting the 5th gives fame and honour of its own.
  • But as the dhana-lord placed 12th from its own house, finances do not run smoothly — shortage comes just when need is high.
  • They take up public service and high position but won't misuse them — honour and dharma are in their blood — yet, considering borrowing a dishonour, Jupiter makes them a debtor, running their work through others.
Teaching note

The other saviour: Jupiter owns the 2nd & 5th (dhana + trikoṇa), a strong benefic. The reading is the honourable, generous 'big man' — but with a money-flow problem (dhana-lord placed awkwardly) that turns them into debtors. A largely good placement with a caveat.

Venus (Śukra)

சுக்கிரன்

Neutral · 7th & 12th lord

  • A fair, moderate state: a pompous, comfortable life becomes a habit they cannot come down from — tasty food, decorative and showy articles a must.
  • The wives they get are also pomp-loving spenders who dominate the home; understanding the native's weakness, the wife wins them and makes them her instrument.
  • Even so, they are ready to leap to the 'next branch,' keeping other-women ties hidden and spending lavishly; their showy appearance draws women around them.
  • As lord of pleasures (and 7th & 12th) in the lagna they must fully enjoy — with real earning strength too: good businessmen, trade-chiefs, commission-agents and farm-owners.
Teaching note

Venus (7th & 12th, neutral) gives the comfort-loving, wife-dominated, pleasure-and-earning reading. The 12th-lordship pulls toward loss and other-women ties. Handle the desire material as the text's framing.

Saturn (Śani)

சனி

Enemy · 3rd & 4th lord

  • The harsh Saturn, an enemy to Scorpio, first breeds carelessness and laziness — plunging in without forethought, starting and dropping things midway.
  • They begin with speed and slow to a crawl, building castles of 'I'll do this' without ever stepping in; as an enemy it gains no strength even in its own houses.
  • As the 4th lord, comfort (sukha) is only imaginary — everything comes through intense struggle; good thoughts don't arise, and what they fear tends to happen.
  • From low self-confidence they despair quickly and miss chances; and cannot be firm in women-matters — a single glance and they fall, careless of looks.
Teaching note

Saturn is a functional enemy here (3rd & 4th) and one of the weakest placements — carelessness, unfinished castles, imaginary comfort, chronic self-doubt. The opposite of Saturn's exalted glory in Libra.

Rāhu (Rāhu)

ராகு

Enemy · (exalted?)

  • A strange state: haste, anger, and getting caught in problems' snares; a hasty, stubborn, quick-angered intellect.
  • With the scorpion for symbol, Rāhu gives a crooked, venomous, malicious mind — mocking anyone, tormenting the wife (stinging through speech and delighting in it).
  • Ready for fights and quarrels; earning is not by honest ways — every crooked path taken without hesitation, 'somehow money must come.'
  • They respect no one, live by 'seeing is believing,' keep ties with low-class women and handle women roughly, knowing nothing of gentleness.
Teaching note

Rāhu (a functional enemy, though said to be exalted here — disputed) intensifies the scorpion's sting into a crooked, venomous mind. Present the harsh material as the text's severe framing of a difficult placement.

Ketu (Ketu)

கேது

Enemy · (exalted?)

  • Worse than Rāhu: of a spiteful mind, they take terrible revenge and never forget those who harm them, reversing cruelly and without mercy.
  • Skilled in mantra, māntrīka and tāntrik works, they take revenge through them; some act as fake sādhus or keep their company.
  • Self-interested masqueraders, expert at winning trust and then 'cutting the throat,' reading others' weaknesses and checkmating them.
  • Very bad in the matter of women — the source's framing here is extreme and indiscriminate; from excess desire the union is rough, yet still unsatisfying.
Teaching note

The harshest single placement in the chapter. Present strictly as the source's severe, moralised characterisation of a difficult, occult-tinged placement — indicative, chart-dependent, never a verdict on a person. Because Scorpio is the 8th (occult) sign, texts pile intensity onto the nodes here.

PART 5

Conjunctions
in Viruccigam

The Sun joined with each planet — and a rare four-planet combine

Moon + Mars + Jupiter (+Sun) → Gaja-Kesari · Chandra-Maṅgala → rāja yoga

Teaching note

The author frames the two-planet combos around the Sun; then the multi-planet combines. Preview the crown jewel: Moon+Mars+Jupiter, which forms Gaja-Kesari, Chandra-Maṅgala and Guru-Maṅgala together — and with the Sun added, a rāja yoga.

CONJUNCTIONS

Sun + Moon · Sun + Mars

Sun + Moon

Not bad to call it: good earning strength and a rising profession, though with frequent mental agitation and un-firm action — yet they try again and win. Family-attached, not miserly, spending as need requires; energetic but of unsound health, so often fatigued; and, even being cautious, they slip into women's net here.

Sun + Mars

Gives extraordinary briskness and high skill-in-action — but haste, anger and fury are their enemies: everything must be done fast, and if it can't be finished right they turn furious, missing chances. They overreach, take needless loans, and by cutting speech make hidden enemies. High pitta; the desire-and-disease pattern repeats.

Teaching note

Sun+Moon = earning and career with mental agitation, but they win through. Sun+Mars = extraordinary drive spoiled by haste and fury, with overreach and hidden enemies. Both carry the sign's desire-and-health caveat.

CONJUNCTIONS

Sun + Mercury · Sun + Jupiter

Sun + Mercury (Budha-Āditya)

Makes them very skilled and capable — everything planned, no rest till the task is done, seeing the profession's finest subtleties and grasping anyone quickly. Being earners, good spenders; fond of the arts and of women, whom their appearance draws — though they keep no permanent ties. Problems keep coming, but they overcome them.

Sun + Jupiter

Gives good — makes them truly big men: fame, influence, professional and earning strength, political ties, public service and divine works, with no self-interest. They spend their own money freely, help all, love great souls and holy places, guard their honour, keep private matters hidden, and do not wander for women though attracted.

Teaching note

Sun+Mercury (Budha-Āditya) is the very-skilled, problem-overcoming one. Sun+Jupiter is the finest of the Sun-conjunctions — two saviours together making a genuine, honourable big man with no self-interest. A strong contrast to the difficult combines to come.

CONJUNCTIONS

Sun + Venus · Sun + Saturn

Sun + Venus

Cannot be faulted: professional and trade skill, good earning strength, and a pompous, comfortable life with nothing lacking — a decorated home, buying whatever keeps the wife beautiful, 'turning night into heaven.' Yet even amid comfort they feel it is not enough and, wondering whether more pleasure lies elsewhere, exceed the wife's control and taste other women.

Sun + Saturn

Cannot be called good — always a conflict in the mind, unable to reach a quick decision, so time passes and chances are lost. The son Saturn spoils the Sun's elevations; the father-relation is poor; excess thinking, hesitation, laziness and mental fatigue attack; they cannot behave truly toward the wife and find no satisfaction. 'Their own mind is their enemy.'

Teaching note

Sun+Venus is the comfortable-but-never-enough, desire-driven one. Sun+Saturn (father-son enemies) is the mind-conflicted, opportunity-losing, joyless one — 'their own mind is their enemy.' Present the desire material as the text's framing.

CONJUNCTIONS

Sun + Rāhu · Sun + Ketu

Sun + Rāhu (or Ketu)

Not special. Both spoil the Sun's good, ruining a lasting, high professional name; honesty is abandoned, and to earn money any means is taken — mostly by crooked ways: speculation, gambling, races and betting, smuggling, intoxicant-goods, cheating and fraud, black-marketing, running prostitution — and possibly mantra/tāntrik work.

The author's summary

'Generally these two nodes with the Sun make the Scorpio native act badly.' Because Rāhu and Ketu are (said to be) exalted in Scorpio and it is a 'mobile/agitated' sign, the nodes can rule the native entirely, as they please.

Teaching note

The nodal conjunctions with the Sun corrupt it — money-at-any-cost by every crooked route. The author notes that, the nodes being (disputedly) exalted here in a 'mobile' sign, they can dominate the Scorpio native entirely. The cautionary end of the Sun-conjunction spectrum. Present descriptively.

CONJUNCTIONS

The multi-planet combines

Moon + Mars + Jupiter — a wondrous combine: Gaja-Kesari, Guru-Maṅgala and Chandra-Maṅgala yogas form together, keeping them very high. Add the Sun → a rāja yoga, and big political position surely comes.

The strong combines

Sun+Moon+Jupiter gives firmness (vairāgya) and success; Moon+Mars gives Neechabhaṅga Rāja Yoga (the fallen Moon redeemed); Moon+Jupiter and Mars+Jupiter give good deeds and honour.

The steady middle

Sun+Moon+Mercury, Sun+Moon+Venus, Moon+Guru+Venus, Sun+Mercury+Guru/Saturn — 'not to be called spoiling': mostly good, comforts and earning intact, social standing created.

The mixed / conflicted

Moon+Mercury/Venus → unsteady, wife dominates; Moon+Mercury+Venus → craving all pleasures; Mars+Mercury/Venus, Mars+Saturn/nodes → no help, 'they do not know the good way.'

The worst combines

Mercury+Jupiter → always in confusion; Venus+Jupiter → masqueraders ('good as fake sādhus'); any combine with Rahu/Ketu → crooked ways and fraud, worst of all.

Teaching note

The crown jewel first: Moon+Mars+Jupiter forms Gaja-Kesari + Chandra-Mangala + Guru-Mangala together, and with the Sun a rāja yoga — the sign's great redemption. Note also Moon+Mars = Neechabhanga Raja Yoga (the fallen Moon lifted). Then the tiers down to the nodal-fraud combines.

SYNTHESIS

Putting it together

"A lagna hemmed in by its own enemies — even the Moon fallen and Mars turned foe — yet carried through by the Sun and Jupiter. In truth, Scorpio natives are capable people."

Scorpio in one line: Mars's intense, secretive fixed-water sign of the scorpion — deeply skilled and penetrating, self-guarded, with the mind fallen and the will fierce.

Lean on the saviours (Sun, Jupiter); watch the Moon's phase for neechabhaṅga; look for the Moon+Mars+Jupiter yoga. Confirm with the whole chart.

Teaching note

Close the arc. The Scorpio lesson: a lagna full of functional enemies (fallen Moon, Mars-as-foe, Saturn, nodes) redeemed by the Sun (10th) and Jupiter (2nd & 5th), and crowned by the Moon+Mars+Jupiter combine. Reiterate: single factors are indicative; confirm with strength, aspects, vargas, daśā — and always check the Moon's phase for neechabhaṅga.

FOUNDATIONS

Dhaṉusu at a glance

Symbol

The bow with a drawn arrow — the archer-centaur, man to the waist, horse below. A four-footed (nāṟkāl) sign.

Element / class

Fire (agni tattva) · saumya (benign) · a dharma rāśi · a dry (waterless) sign · elevated (uyarnda)

Modality

Ubhaya (உபய · dual) · Ōja (odd) · male sign · single-bodied

Order & body

9th sign of the zodiac · signifies the thighs and hips (kudam)

Ruler / dignities

Jupiter (குரு) — a full benefic, holding mūlatrikoṇa here · no planet debilitated

Direction / colour

East (கிழக்கு) · yellow (some say light-red) · strong by day in the first 15°, by night in the last 15°

Speed and valour — the arrow that flies straight to its mark. Foreign scholars call it “Thaishikam.”
Teaching note

Dual signs split in two halves; Sagittarius is strong by day in its first 15° and by night in its last 15°.

Two yogakārakas

The Sun (9th, bhāgya) — a full benefic — and Mars (5th, trikoṇa). Jupiter, lord of the 1st and 4th, takes kendrādhipati-doṣa in a kendra other than the Lagna.

Māraka — 7th & 11th ⚠

Mercury (7th) and Venus (11th) are the author's mārakādhipatis. The textbook māraka houses are the 2nd & 7th — teach both.

Bādhaka — 7th

Dual signs take the 7th as bādhaka; Mercury is bādhakādhipati. Mercury and Venus are the functional malefics.

The 8th lord is a friend

The Moon owns the 8th (āyus) but is friendly to the Lagna — so it increases longevity.

Teaching note

The 11th as māraka is the author's own departure; the bādhaka (7th, Mercury) is the standard dual-sign rule.

FOUNDATIONS

What Dhaṉusu governs (kārakatva)

Learning & dharma

Temples, Veda-schools, homam and yajña places, priests (archakar), professors; multilingual scholarship; ashrams, sannyāsa, maṭhas and trusts

Office & authority

Government offices, police, security, the army; keeping arms; political ties and high authority; economists and planners; public-service bodies

Money & word

Banking, treasury, money-lending and chit-funds; newspapers, journalism, editing and reporting; the postal department

Body & health

The thighs and hips — diseases of that region, especially piles (mūla-vyādhi). Also sports experts and trainers.

FOUNDATIONS

Places ruled by Dhaṉusu

Within Bhārat

Tirupati · Travancore · Hampi · Chittoor · Vellore · Senji · Mysore · Srirangapatnam and fort-towns · the Thungabhadra banks · the southern coasts of Tamil Nadu and Kerala

Around the world

South Sri Lanka · central Europe · Arabia · Afghanistan · war-zones · armouries and weapon-training grounds (āyudha-sālai)

Forts, armouries, temples and government seats — the theme is the bow: valour, law and dharma.
THE NATIVE'S PORTRAIT

Classical view · two authorities

Phaladīpikā

A long neck, an ill-shaped face, big ears and a big nose; strives in his tasks; dear to the king; clean teeth; charitable; destroys his enemies; won over only by loving speech; strong.

Satyāchārya

Long neck, teeth and nose; kapha-vāta body; tall male build; fierce in action; valorous; loses wealth to the government (theft, fire); learned in a fine profession and the śāstras; respected by many; travels to many lands and earns; mediator in dharma matters; measured attachment to the wife.

THE NATIVE'S PORTRAIT

Classical view · Jātaka Alaṅkāram

Fears his own siblings; of great fame and glory; skilled in women's pleasure; conquers those who oppose him; of much learning; wealthy from a young age — but in middle age suffers difficulties and weaknesses.

THE NATIVE'S PORTRAIT

From experience · the arrow to the target

Straight to the mark

As an arrow set to the bow flies straight, he carries a task to completion at speed. Thinks well, decides — and once decided, does not change.

Pure word

Once he gives his word he will not draw back, accepting whatever hardship or loss to fulfil it.

Magnanimous & learned

Keeps away from petty matters; never surrenders his honour; deep learning, even without schooling. Many seek his counsel — a town-elder whose word is obeyed.

Conscience is his master

Speaks openly; admits a real fault but never a false blame; questions injustice even in government — and fights for the workers and the downtrodden, from the soul, not for show.

THE NATIVE'S PORTRAIT

From experience · family, money, freedom

A cool marriage

Loves his family, but the wife does not quite suit him — less warmth until middle age, more in later life. (The lore: Jupiter dislikes Mercury, the 7th lord, born of the Moon and Tārā.)

Careful with money

Very careful — especially with public money; strict, even harsh, with his own children.

More daughters

Mostly gets more daughters, on whom his affection falls — yet thrifty with the children and quick to see small faults.

Bound only by love

“He cannot be bound by wealth or authority — only by love, affection and good friendship.” Fame and position seek him out; he fulfils any duty without error.

Jupiter (Guru)

குரு
Lord · own sign · mūlatrikoṇa

The big man at home.

  • A big-man bearing of its own; others connect with awe-mixed respect, and his word is obeyed by many.
  • A little lazy — gets others to do what is needed and to bear his expenses; even a gift is arranged in his name. Very thrifty.
  • Mostly more daughters, with more affection for them; gives what they ask — yet thrifty toward the children.
  • Public service, divine works, political ties, headmanship and the panchayat seek him; modest and reserved in women-matters, keeping life honourable.

Sun (Sūrya)

சூரியன்
Yogakāraka · 9th lord

An elevated state — a truly big man.

  • An excellent, brisk administrator who works fast “as if a wheel were fixed to his leg”; others can't keep his pace.
  • Where others err he quietly sets it right rather than pointing it out — so they hold him in awe-mixed respect.
  • Good earning strength, not miserly; dislikes having others spend for him; keen in public service; hard to deceive.
  • Always cautious and well-planned; devoted to family, elders and great souls; desire present, but no wandering.

Moon (Candra)

சந்திரன்
Friend · 8th lord

The 8th lord as a friend — longevity rises.

  • As 8th (āyus) lord but a friend of the Lagna, the Moon increases longevity.
  • Waxing Moon: mind-firmness and skill rise — fearless, quick to act, respected, generous, attached to mother, elders and relatives.
  • Waning Moon: less special — careless, impatient and thrifty; lets others bear the expenses; thinks many times over even needful family spending.
  • Slippery in women-matters — coaxing the wife until a task is done, then slipping away.

Mars (Kuja)

செவ்வாய்
Yogakāraka · 5th & 12th

A distinguished, special state.

  • Fame and influence; a little quick to anger, but magnanimous; speed and discernment; an excellent administrator, keen on sports.
  • Political ties, public service and divine works — with no self-interest or profit-seeking.
  • Honour-conscious; a generous spender; respects siblings and elders; fearless, speaking his mind openly and unworried by the trouble it brings.
  • Mostly reaches high positions — administrators, big businessmen, real-estate, factory-owners; a dignified life.

Mercury (Budha)

புதன்
Bādhaka · māraka · 7th & 10th

Slippery — but strong in the profession.

  • Win a strong professional position; take up work of little physical labour, getting it done through others while supervising all.
  • Not easily deceived, very cautious; good taste in arts, mathematics and jyotiṣa — “a good merchant,” calculating even small matters.
  • The wife dominates the home, but they keep other-women ties; skilled in the art of love.
  • Clever, sweet talkers who rarely let others speak — “nothing is impossible for us”; mostly make life comfortable.

Venus (Śukra)

சுக்கிரன்
Functional malefic · 6th & 11th

The malefic lord subjugates the Lagna.

  • Venus in the Lagna makes them act as it wills: pompous living and big spending that sinks the sign's glory.
  • Hesitant to borrow, yet become debtors through random spending; unable to keep their word; hidden enemies.
  • Ties with women, possibly a second household; for women they will spend anything — youth's pleasures turning into old age's sufferings.
  • Natural taste in the arts, but misdirected; income comes, yet shortage follows and later life turns sorrowful.

Saturn (Śani)

சனி
2nd & 3rd lord

Not special — it spoils the glory of Sagittarius.

  • Breeds laziness, carelessness and lack of self-confidence; discernment of good and bad reduces.
  • Acts with eyes closed, without forethought — mismanages and loses in the profession; low company and crooked money-seeking that ends in failure.
  • Very bad in women-matters: as the 3rd (bhoga) lord, enjoyment-thoughts run high even with a beautiful wife.
  • Health declines from excess — spinal trouble (spondylitis) and piles (mūla-vyādhi) attack severely.

Rahu (Rāhu)

ராகு
Friend · Kōthaṇḍa Rāhu

The bow-Rāhu — a special arrangement.

  • Jyotiṣa marks Rāhu in Sagittarius as special: Kōthaṇḍa Rāhu — it acts excellently in its daśā/bhukti.
  • Good subject-knowledge and skill; the power to complete tasks fast and precisely; darśan and ties with great souls.
  • Political and public-service ties with no profit-seeking; dharma-minded and helpful; ties with jñānis and mahāns.
  • Even with desire, conduct that guards their honour; family-attached. “He is called the arrow shot from the bow.”

Ketu (Ketu)

கேது
Friend

Immense intellect, a spiritual bent.

  • Not equal to Rāhu's state, yet immense intellect and jñāna — a camphor-intellect that grasps anything at once.
  • Ties with jñānis, mahāns, monasteries and divine works; take the front rank of their own accord; fulfil any responsibility precisely.
  • Keen in bhakti, discourse and upanyāsa, dhyāna and yoga; fond of homam and yajña; ties with māntrik and tāntrik practitioners.
  • Only in women-matters do they act somewhat badly; good to the good, very bad to those who oppose them.
PART 5

Conjunctions
in Dhaṉusu

The Sun — the bhāgya-lord and full benefic — joined with each planet

Dharma-Karmādhipati · Kōthaṇḍa Rāhu · the two yogakārakas together

CONJUNCTIONS

Sun + Moon · Sun + Mars

Sun + Moon

Special. The Moon drops its 8th-lord blemish — good administrators of earning strength; political and public-service involvement without profit-seeking; dharma-minded, with ties to jñānis and mahāns; even with desire, they guard their honour.

Sun + Mars — a wondrous arrangement

The two yogakārakas together raise them very high: big businessmen, trade-chiefs, holders of authority and political position. Public and dharma works without accounting; front-line for the people's grievances — fighting even the government, opposing bribery; the means to build wells, orphanages, ashrams and temples.

CONJUNCTIONS

Sun + Mercury · Sun + Jupiter

Sun + Mercury — Dharma-Karmādhipati yoga

The 9th (dharma) and 10th (karma) lords together keep life high and noble. A camphor-intellect — accounting expert plus jyotiṣa, vedānta and tattva; a comfortable life troubles do not approach; born to enjoy and to help others.

Sun + Jupiter

Without doubt special — the Lagna-lord and the bhāgya-lord make very big men: high status, authority and political position; professional rise; keen in public service, monastery and temple-service; taking no profit; honoured by the public, never doing a dishonourable act.

CONJUNCTIONS

Sun + Venus · Sun + Saturn

Sun + Venus

Not special. Venus is a functional enemy of this Lagna and of the Sun. Combust, its marriage-qualities reduce; not combust, it makes them eye others' wives, act small-mindedly and pompously — reaping trouble and disease from random spending.

Sun + Saturn

Not satisfying. The father–son relation is poor; good qualities reduce; laziness and delay spoil chances; low company and labour-less money-seeking; endless spending and shortage; very bad in women-matters.

CONJUNCTIONS

Sun + Rāhu · Sun + Ketu

Sun + Rāhu — wondrous here

Generally Sun+Rāhu spoils the Sun — but in Sagittarius the Sun keeps its glory, because this is Kōthaṇḍa Rāhu. Good jñāna and interest in vedānta, tattva, bhakti, dhyāna, yoga and kuṇḍalinī; keen in public service, ashram and monastery.

Sun + Ketu

Not special. Ketu reduces the Sun's glory, but they have immense intellect — cautious, escaping any tangle by cunning; thrifty, getting expenses done by others; clever, tall-talking and proud.

CONJUNCTIONS

The multi-planet combines

The crowning combine

Sun + Mars + Jupiter — wondrous achievers, a very big yoga: the leadership-seat, guiding others rightly, with wealth, status and high position. Sun+Mars+Moon and Sun+Mars+Mercury also act well, giving elevations.

The favourable combines

Sun+Mercury+Jupiter gives good results; Sun+Guru+Rāhu makes achievers (with Ketu, fulfilment on the bhakti-path); Jupiter+Moon+Mars gives distinction; Moon+Mars is good — firm-minded, devout, generous.

The mixed combines

Sun+Mars+Venus forms well but brings tāra-doṣa (a second marriage possible); Sun+Guru+Saturn and Sun+Mars+Rāhu are moderate; Moon+Jupiter is moderate — magnanimous but of changing mind.

The weak combines

Sun+Mars+Saturn struggles; Moon+Mercury and Moon+Venus breed big spending; Moon+Saturn (worse with Ketu) brings low company and crooked ways; Mercury/Venus/Saturn with the nodes — none satisfying.

SYNTHESIS

Putting it together

“He cannot be bound by wealth or authority — only by love, affection and good friendship.”

Sagittarius in one line: Jupiter's fire of dharma — no enemy, no fall — loosed like an arrow by two yogakārakas, the Sun and Mars.

Its defining yogas: Sun+Mars (two trine-lords), Sun+Mercury (Dharma-Karmādhipati) and Kōthaṇḍa Rāhu. Confirm with the whole chart.

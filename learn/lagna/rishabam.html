Teaching note

Welcome back to the series. Chapter 2 is the Rishabam (Taurus) Ascendant. Set the frame: where Mesham was fire (Sun exalted, Mars ruler), Rishabam is its water counterpart — Moon exalted, Venus ruler. Same study method as last week; a calmer, earthier temperament. As always, single-factor readings are indicative, confirmed against the full chart.

FOUNDATIONS

Rishabam at a glance

Symbol

எருது — the Bull / Ox (a quadruped). Hence 'Taurus'.

Element / class

Earth-natured, water-ruled · saumya (benign) sign

Modality

Sthira (ஸ்திர · fixed) · Yugma (யுக்ம · even) · female sign

Order & body

2nd sign of the zodiac · signifies the face (முகம் · mukha)

Ruler / exaltation

Venus (சுக்கிரன்) rules · Moon (சந்திரன்) is exalted here

Direction / colour

South (தென்) · White (வெண்மை) · strong at night · pṛṣṭhodaya

The water-sign mirror of Mesham: where Aries paired two fire planets, Taurus pairs two water planets — Moon exalted, Venus ruling.
Teaching note

Anchor the image: a bull, farmland, the face, the second sign — patient, sensual, steady. Draw the elegant parallel the author loves: Mesham = fire pair (Sun uccha, Mars lord); Rishabam = water pair (Moon uccha, Venus lord). Fixed + earth + Venus already predicts stability, comfort-seeking, and love of beauty.

Teaching note

The backbone table. Star fact: Saturn rules BOTH the 9th (dharma) and 10th (karma) — the classic Taurus yogakāraka, and the only sign where one planet owns both trines-of-action. Mercury and Saturn are the friends/yogakārakas; Sun and Jupiter are enemies here; the nodes debilitate. Predict from this which single planets shine (Venus, Moon-waxing, Mercury, Saturn).

Yogakāraka

Saturn (9th+10th) is the great yogakāraka; Mercury (2nd+5th) is a benefic yogakāraka too.

Bādhaka — 9th

Fixed signs take the 9th as bādhaka; Saturn is bādhakādhipati, but as a yogakāraka the doṣa is much reduced.

Māraka — author's note

The author names the 3rd & 8th (Moon, Jupiter) as māraka here. Standard texts use the 2nd & 7th (Mercury, Mars) — teach both.

Teaching note

Have students internalise the lordships — every later judgment uses them. Two teaching points: (1) Saturn as sole 9+10 lord is why Taurus is considered fortunate for career-with-dharma. (2) Be honest that the author's māraka assignment (3 & 8) departs from the textbook 2 & 7 — a good moment on how traditions vary. Bādhaka = 9th is correct for a fixed sign.

Teaching note

The through-line is Venus + earth: land, dairy, beauty, the arts, and the pleasures of the senses — very different from Mesham's fire and metal. Note the strong 'women / textiles / arts / hotels' cluster for career reading, and the kapha/respiratory signature for health, both of which recur in the planet sections.

Greek/Latin name Tāvuri (Tauros / Taurus, "the bull") — noted by Varāhamihira in the Bṛhat Jātaka, carried into Jyotiṣa from the Yavana tradition. The theme is fertile, crop-rich land.
Teaching note

Used in mundane and relocation work. The common thread is fertile agricultural land — fitting for the sign of the bull and the fields. The Tāvuri/Taurus note pairs with last week's Krios/Kriya: our zodiac names travelled from Greece into Sanskrit.

THE NATIVE'S PORTRAIT

Classical view · two authorities

Phaladīpikā (Mantreśvara)

Broad thighs & face; inclined to farming; comfortable from mid-life on; fond of women; charitable; patient; fond of tending cattle; a mole on the seat (pṛṣṭhodaya).

Satyāchārya

Thick lips, wide forehead; kapha-vāta body; a giver; big spender; few sons, more daughters; ever eager at work; wealthy; devoted to the wife; hates weapons; gives to relatives.

Teaching note

Read these as period portraits. Both agree on the earthy, comfort-loving, generous, family-devoted core — very Venusian/Taurine. Point out the recurring 'farming, cattle, patience, wealth, devotion to wife' motifs; contrast with Mesham's fiery restlessness.

THE NATIVE'S PORTRAIT

Classical view · Jātaka Alaṅkāram

Worships ascetics; guards truth; eats sparingly; loves sour taste; stout build; does allotted work; keen on jyotiṣa; collects ornaments; small ears; subtle intellect; a cough ailment; many children.

THE NATIVE'S PORTRAIT

From experience · temperament

Earth-like patience

Deliberate and unhurried; think before acting; never hasty. Even resentment stays hidden.

Family at the centre

Deep attachment to family; charitable to dāna, dharma and temple works; the wife's hand somewhat rules the home.

Discreet desire

Strong desire and fondness for women — but any outside connection is cleverly, secretly concealed from the wife.

Comfort & beauty

Not miserly; good spenders, especially on women, clothes, ornaments, perfumes; always neat and stylish.

Teaching note

The author's own synthesis — the most useful portrait. The Taurus signature: patient, steady, home-and-comfort-centred, aesthetically refined, privately sensual. Frame kindly as tendencies modified by conjunction/aspect. Contrast sharply with Mesham's impulsive fire.

THE NATIVE'S PORTRAIT

From experience · life, pleasures, health

Peace-loving

Emotionally tender; dislike quarrels and litigation; get along with neighbours; will pay to end trouble quickly.

'My own work'

Keep out of others' affairs; low taste for public service — but wholehearted in divine matters; finish any duty well.

Sensual & steady

Cool constitution — can sustain long in union; women are drawn to them; fond of pets, cool foods, sour tastes; dislike travel.

Health watch

Facial pimples, scars, blemishes; kapha & respiratory disease — cough, sinus, pneumonia; ~75% risk of women-related illness.

Teaching note

Handle the sensual material maturely — it's the text's framing of a cool, enduring constitution, not a moral claim. The health card gathers the kapha/respiratory signature and the facial theme (Taurus = the face). Note the homebody streak: achieve through others, from one place.

Venus (Śukra)

சுக்கிரன்
Lord · 1st & 6th

The lord at home — pleasure, and a debt-catch.

  • Lord of pleasures in its own sign: an unquenchable drive to taste every worldly comfort; always well-dressed, attractive, perfumed.
  • Deep attachment, especially to daughters and women; a comfortable life is assured; prefers to act through others.
  • But also 6th lord (enemies, debt, disease) — some ailment always present; kapha, cough, lung trouble; covert enemies (overcome).
  • Luxury-love → big spender who borrows freely; wife dominates the home (Venus = Lagna lord + spouse-kāraka). In a female chart, she rules the husband.
Teaching note

Venus is the whole personality of this sign — pleasure, beauty, relationship. The teaching nuance: it also rules the 6th, so debt and minor chronic illness shadow the comfort. The spouse-dominance theme recurs whenever Venus or the 7th is involved.

Sun (Sūrya)

சூரியன்
Enemy · 4th lord

An enemy — yet good here.

  • As 4th lord it is an enemy of the Lagna, but a malefic may sit in a kendra.
  • Clever reasoning: Rishabam is the 10th from the Sun's own 4th (Leo) — the very house where the Sun gets Dik bala — so it fully delivers its 4th-house good.
  • Power to earn fast; comfortable, modern, luxurious homes; responsible posts with political/government connection, even unsought.
  • Acts well without chasing profit; spends on good deeds and charity; self-motivated. Sun alone here gives only good results.
Teaching note

Great worked example of positional reasoning: an 'enemy' planet turns beneficial because of where it sits (10th from its own house = Dik bala). Teach students to trace these relationships rather than label a planet good/bad.

Moon (Candra)

சந்திரன்

Exalted · 3rd lord

  • Exalted 3rd lord (courage, enterprise, victory) — with a waxing Moon, works turn out well; they act with courage and succeed.
  • Always neat and disciplined; discharge duty precisely; get things done cleverly through others, especially women.
  • Reconcile with a smile, never harsh; skilled at winning women over; savour worldly pleasures and women.
  • With the exalted 3rd-lord in the Lagna, remarkable skill in union — unforgettable to partners; 'but they leap from flower to flower.'
Teaching note

Moon is exalted here, so this is a strong, attractive placement — but check the pakṣa (waxing vs waning) first, always. The 'flower to flower' line captures the sensual-but-restless-in-love shadow. This mirrors the waxing/waning lesson from Mesham.

Mars (Kuja)

செவ்வாய்

Neutral · 7th & 12th

  • Lord of the 7th (spouse) and 12th (expenditure); as 12th-lord in the Lagna it dominates the Lagna-lord.
  • The wife's rule prevails at home — a hot-tempered, big-spending wife who can push the native into debt.
  • Restless desire, never fully satisfied; other-women connections the wife may discover — for he cannot hide, and sudden anger exposes him.
  • As lord of the reproductive-house in the Lagna: women-related diseases likelier; headache, facial cuts, burns, heat-disorders.
Teaching note

Mars is neutral to Taurus but rules the 7th and 12th, so it lands squarely on marriage and spending. The inability to conceal (sudden anger) is why the native ends up subdued at home — a nice character-mechanics point.

Mercury (Budha)

புதன்

Friend · 2nd & 5th · yogakāraka

  • Dhana (2nd) & pañcama (5th) lord in the Lagna: immense sharpness of intellect and cleverness; thinks things through, revises wrong decisions.
  • Somewhat thrifty — liberal only on necessary expense; careful about savings; mindful of honour, so acts cautiously.
  • Inclination to public service & divine matters; important responsibility comes and is discharged well, so fame follows.
  • Reluctant to borrow, yet money still comes; charitable after judging worthiness. Gets Dik bala in the Lagna as a śubha yogakāraka — special results.
Teaching note

Mercury is a benefic yogakāraka here (owns the 5th trine) and gets Dik bala in the 1st — a genuinely strong, dignified placement, quite unlike Mercury-in-Mesham. Emphasise the thrift + honour + savings profile.

Jupiter (Guru)

குரு

Enemy · 8th & 11th

  • 8th & 11th lord; gets Dik bala in the Lagna → good longevity strength, but makes them very lazy.
  • Skilled at getting work done through others while doing nothing; a proper Āṣāḍha-bhūti (hypocrite) — noble in public, different in private; double-faced.
  • Stingy 'lords' who profit from others' spending and labour, gaining fame and influence; gossip & lawsuits come, but they manage — even become village headman.
  • 8th & 11th are livelihood houses, so money flows freely; some become fake swāmis; very bad but covert with women.
Teaching note

Jupiter is an ENEMY for Taurus (rules 8 & 11) — the opposite of its role in Mesham. Hence the unflattering 'hypocrite / stingy lord / fake swāmi' portrait. Good lesson that the same benefic planet plays very differently by sign-lordship.

Saturn (Śani)

சனி

Yogakāraka · 9th & 10th

  • Dharmakarmādhipati (9th + 10th) — a benefic yogakāraka; the finest of yogas, unique to Taurus as one planet.
  • Abundant guidance and support from the father; deep respect and affection toward him; love of divine works and pilgrimages.
  • God-belief, modesty, quiet charity — yet fame seeks them; many reach high professional standing and become big industry magnates.
  • The dread of Saturn does NOT apply here: even 7½-Saturn, aṣṭama & ardhāṣṭama Saturn do no harm in the Lagna; women-ties kept hidden; nerve/knee vāta possible.
Teaching note

The headline placement of the chapter. Saturn — usually feared — is the great yogakāraka for Taurus, so even its dreaded transits are benign here. This is why Taurus natives with strong Saturn rise in career with a dharmic reputation. Contrast with debilitated Saturn in Mesham.

Rahu (Rāhu)

ராகு
Debilitated

Debilitated — and satisfying.

  • Nīca in Rishabam, and a friend of Saturn whose sign this is — so its workings are satisfying.
  • Covert and hard to read; reveal nothing; even their occupation is hard to discover; earn well, spend well, love luxury.
  • Help others only after sizing them up; cut off those they dislike; not easily deceived; escape any tangle cleverly.
  • Hide their god-belief; drawn to fierce-deity worship, mantra & tantra; connections with low-class women; may profit through widows' ties.
Teaching note

Nodal debilitation is tradition-dependent (some schools exalt Rahu in Taurus) — flag that. In the author's system, debilitated-but-Saturn-friendly Rahu behaves usefully here: shrewd, self-protective, materially successful, with an occult undercurrent.

Ketu (Ketu)

கேது
Debilitated

Subtle knowledge — and an occult edge.

  • Immense subtle knowledge (sūkṣma-jñāna); grasp anything at once; handle any problem skilfully, so others seek their help.
  • A riddle — hard to tell good from bad; but if trusted, they surely do good. Do not make an enemy of them — they can destroy enemies by any means.
  • Know fierce-deity worship, mantra, tantra — others are awed or deceived; money must come, but once it comes they won't spend freely.
  • Very bad with women (charm them to their will); can pose as fake swāmis; rich in philosophy, vedānta, logic; their mood can turn without warning.
Teaching note

Ketu here is the sharp, esoteric, hard-to-read type. Balance the warning with the upside: subtle intellect, problem-solving, genuine help when trusted. Note the recurring occult (mantra-tantra) motif across both nodes for Taurus.

PART 5

Conjunctions
in Rishabam

Kūṭṭu graha — Venus joined with each planet, and the multi-planet combines

…and why too many planets in one house is itself a problem (graha-yuddha)

Teaching note

Because Venus is the lord, the author frames the combinations as 'Venus with X'. Warn first about the Sun's nearness to Venus — combustion/retrogression changes the reading, covered on the next slide.

CONJUNCTIONS

Venus + Sun (and the combustion caution)

First, a caution: the Sun orbits close to Venus, so in Rishabam it often falls into retrogression, combustion (astamana), or retro-combustion. Combust → Venus dims and the Sun acts; retrograde → strength rises; retro-combust → "a cat on the fence," unable to decide.

Venus + Sun

Born enemies → mixed good & bad. Water meets fire, so the body stays uneven; unsatisfying union may bring other-women ties and delayed marriage, with frequent friction with the wife. Earning-minded yet big-spending; political & public-service leanings; wants to be foremost. Not lazy, but frequent heat-disorders, kapha diseases, indigestion, ulcer, appendicitis.

Teaching note

Teach the combustion/retrograde caution as a general Venus-Sun principle, not just for Taurus. Then the Venus+Sun reading: fire-water clash → bodily unevenness and marriage friction, but public ambition. This is why the Sun near Venus always needs care.

CONJUNCTIONS

Venus + Moon — the two faces

+ Waxing Moon — special

Two water planets, one ruling, one exalted. In a female chart, a rāja-kumārī yoga. Attractive appearance draws women; great pleasure and income through them; strong bonds with mother, sister, daughters; high status, luxury homes, vehicles, servants, jewellery; firm mind achieves anything.

+ Waning Moon — reversed

Unsteady, fickle mind; things left half-done; bad with women. Spends lavishly (even on debt) to win praise; lazy, careless about earning; squanders ancestral property; invites needless trouble; some ailment recurs, with no care for treatment.

Same conjunction, opposite lives — the Moon's phase decides.
Teaching note

The clearest waxing/waning contrast in the chapter, and with two water planets (one exalted) the waxing case is outstanding — even a rāja-kumārī yoga for women. Always check the Moon's phase first. Mirrors the Moon lesson from both chapters.

CONJUNCTIONS

Venus + Mars · Venus + Mercury

Venus + Mars

Knotty: Mars in the Lagna creates family strife — a dominance-struggle, constant quarrels, a big-spending wife driving them to other women (some keep a mistress). Very bad with women; take pleasure even with the worst partner and fall ill. In a female chart, well-hidden affairs. Creates uterine (women) and reproductive/base diseases (men); rough, hasty handling.

Venus + Mercury

Excellent! Influence, eloquence, fame, status, honour, comfort; fine artistic taste; an attractive face that draws women; enjoy pleasures; work with care in public & divine matters and earn fame; good earners free with charity; ancestral property; fond of luxury, art, ornaments; a life without lack and a helpful nature.

Teaching note

A clean contrast: Mars (7th/12th lord) drags Venus into marital strife and health trouble; Mercury (friend/yogakāraka) lifts Venus into art, fame and prosperity. Reinforces that the co-tenant's lordship decides the flavour.

CONJUNCTIONS

Venus + Jupiter · Venus + Saturn

Venus + Jupiter

Strange: the 8th-lord Jupiter loses its enmity/harm; getting Dik bala and sitting 3rd from the 11th, it takes on the 11th-house benefit and raises the native to a respected, advice-giving position — getting work done on others' money. Yet a proper Āṣāḍha-bhūti: a double life, hidden fickleness with women; a life without lack.

Venus + Saturn

Excellent: the dharma-karma lord with its friend Venus — high professional distinction and many businesses, achieved from one place. A little lazy but works hard when needed; escapes entanglements; amiable with staff; public & divine involvement; quiet charity, pilgrimages. Fickle with women (others seem sweeter than the wife) but calm and unbothered; nerve slackness likely.

Teaching note

Venus+Saturn is a standout (two friends, one the great yogakāraka) — career-and-dharma strength. Venus+Jupiter is subtler: the enemy Jupiter is defanged and even helpful, but the double-life shadow remains. Great illustration of house-position rescuing an enemy planet.

CONJUNCTIONS

Venus + Rahu · Venus + Ketu

Venus + Rahu

Not very special: they know a great deal and can't be deceived; money by any means, mostly crooked; even charity expects a return; very miserly — think ten times over expenses; no regard for justice or honesty; get women's company free while posing as generous 'lords of charity'. Keep away.

Venus + Ketu

Many times worse: skilled swindlers, pure self-servers; know every bad path and escape cleverly; covert anti-government deeds; mantra-tantra used for self and to harm; some are fake swāmis calling themselves mahān/siddha/yogi. Very bad with women — enslave them by mantra-craft. Be very cautious.

Teaching note

The difficult nodal conjunctions — materially shrewd but ethically hollow, with a false-guru shadow (Ketu worse). Present descriptively. These are the cautionary end of the Venus-conjunction spectrum, opposite the Venus+Mercury/Saturn brilliance.

CONJUNCTIONS

The multi-planet combines

The bright combines

Venus + Sun + waxing Moon (add Mercury → very high). Add Saturn to those four → a big-business tycoon (rare). Venus + Saturn + Mercury is a 'boon-state' for Taurus. Venus+Mercury+Jupiter, Venus+Moon+Mercury/Jupiter — good with a waxing Moon.

The difficult combines

Venus + Sun, and Sun+Mars+Venus+Rahu/Ketu — sure trouble. Venus+Jupiter+Rahu/Ketu → a covert-wealth 'crooked-path' yoga. Venus+Sun/Mars+Saturn → wife-problems & honour-loss via other women. Venus+Mars+Mercury spoils character.

Graha-yuddha — too many in one house
More than a few planets together create planetary war; results then hinge on each planet's victory/defeat and combustion with the Sun. Beyond four planets in one house, judge very carefully.
Teaching note

Summarise the author's combine guidance. Headline: Venus+Saturn+Mercury is the boon-state for Taurus; a waxing Moon rescues many groupings. Introduce graha-yuddha (planetary war) as the reason 'more planets' isn't 'more good'. This sets up the synthesis slide.

SYNTHESIS

Putting it together

"The conjunction of the other planets must be observed precisely — and the single-planet placements kept in mind — before deciding."

Taurus in one line: patient, sensual, Venus-ruled earth — steadied by a uniquely placed Saturn (9+10) and an exalted Moon.

As with every sign, weigh strength, aspects, vargas and daśā — the Lagna-only reading is the start, not the verdict.

Teaching note

Close the analytic arc. Echo last week's discipline: single-factor readings are indicative; confirm with the whole chart. Reiterate the two structural gifts of Taurus — Saturn as sole dharma-karma lord, and the exalted Moon.
